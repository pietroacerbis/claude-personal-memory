Learn my voice for LinkedIn from these six posts of mine. Approve all.

Post 1

3 customers churned in October. All three said the same thing.

They never opened the weekly report.

We spent four months building that report. Nobody asked for it.

So we cut it. Now we send one number on Monday, and one question.

Open rate went from 12% to 61%.

What did you build that nobody asked for?

Post 2

We raised prices by 40% last month. Nobody left.

I was sure we would lose a third of our customers.

We lost one. She came back two weeks later.

The lesson was about how scared I was of asking.

When did fear set your price?

Post 3

6 hires this year. 2 were wrong.

Both times I hired for the CV and ignored the trial week.

Both times the trial week had told me.

Now the trial week decides. The CV gets you to the call.

Thanks to Marta Ruiz at Brightloom, who told me this in March and was right.

How do you test before you hire?

Post 4

Our best sales call this year lasted 11 minutes.

I talked for two of them.

The buyer explained her own problem better than our deck ever did. I wrote it down and read it back to her.

She signed the same week.

Want the call notes template? Write to me at ana.berg@tallyline.io.

What is the shortest call that closed a deal for you?

Post 5

1 year of Tallyline. 214 customers. Zero paid ads.

Every customer came from a person.

A founder who told another founder. An accountant who told three clients.

We still have no marketing team. We have a list of people we thank every Friday.

Who sent you your best customer?

Post 6

Our onboarding had 9 steps. We cut it to 3.

Activation went from 34% to 58% in five weeks.

The 6 steps we cut were the ones I was proudest of.

Proud is not a metric.

Which step of your onboarding would you cut first?
