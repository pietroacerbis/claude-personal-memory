# 01-workstreams

The areas of work you come back to every week, with no end date: sales, product, content. A piece of work that starts and ends is a project, in `02-projects/`.

Every area has the same shape, built by `SETUP.md`:

| Folder | Holds |
|---|---|
| `01-knowledge/` | what governs the area: your notes, references, the facts Claude needs here |
| `02-artifacts/` | what the area produced |
| `03-archive/` | what retired, kept for the record |

The area's own `README.md` says what it is for, in one line.
